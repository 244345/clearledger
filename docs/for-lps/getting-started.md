# Getting Started as a Liquidity Provider

ClearLedger is an experimental Stellar testnet prototype. Use testnet assets only, and review [pool returns and risks](understanding-yield.md) before interacting.

You will need a Stellar-compatible wallet such as [Freighter](https://freighter.app), a Testnet account with XLM for fees, and Testnet USDC for pool transactions. Never use a mainnet wallet or real funds for this preview.

## Review the pool

Open the Liquidity Provider Portal from the local app and connect Freighter on Testnet. Review pool activity, invoice terms and utilization before deciding whether to deposit. Depositing USDC does not guarantee that an invoice will be funded or that the pool will earn a return.

## Deposit and withdraw

Enter an amount in **Deposit USDC**, review the transaction details and authorize it in Freighter. Deposited USDC becomes part of the pool and may be used to fund eligible invoices under protocol rules. It is not automatically assigned to a specific invoice.

Withdrawals redeem pool shares and depend on unutilized liquidity. If capital is deployed or an invoice is overdue, immediate withdrawal may be limited. See [deposit and withdrawal mechanics](deposit-usdc.md) and [pool utilization](../protocol/liquidity-pool.md#withdrawal).
