# The Problem

Businesses often wait for payment after delivering goods or services. That delay can leave working capital tied up in receivables while the business still needs to pay suppliers, staff and operating costs. Smaller firms may have fewer financing options, and invoice finance can involve fragmented documents, manual reviews and limited visibility into settlement.

## How ClearLedger approaches it

ClearLedger models invoice finance as a shared workflow among the issuer, buyer and liquidity provider. Invoice terms, participant roles, delivery confirmation and repayment are represented in Soroban contract state. A shared pool can fund an eligible listed invoice when protocol rules and available liquidity allow.

The protocol makes transaction state easier to inspect, but it does not replace commercial due diligence, independently validate off-chain documents or ensure that a buyer will repay. It is an experimental testnet project, not a production financing service.
